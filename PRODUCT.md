# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Home gardeners — mostly casual, weekend growers, balcony and veggie-patch enthusiasts who want their garden to thrive without needing a horticulture degree. They respond to clarity and confidence. They buy with emotion but justify with science.

## Product Purpose

BioGardeners sells precision-formulated garden soil and fertilisers direct-to-consumer via a Shopify headless storefront. Success looks like: a product page that communicates trustworthiness and expertise quickly, an easy path to purchase, and a brand experience that makes buyers feel smart for choosing us.

## Brand Personality

Premium · Modern · Precise

Voice: confident and knowledgeable without being academic. We respect the customer's intelligence. We explain the why, not just the what.

## Anti-references

- Generic eco/green brands: no leaf logos, no lime-green palettes, no "natural" copywriting clichés
- Big-box hardware stores: no discount-first messaging, no red/orange utilitarian aesthetic
- Cottage garden / craft aesthetics: no watercolour, no cursive, no "handmade with love"

## Design Principles

1. **Science earns the sale** — every visual and copy choice should feel backed by evidence, not by vibes
2. **Respect the intelligence** — write for people who read labels and ask questions; don't oversimplify
3. **Precision over abundance** — fewer, better choices; one strong visual beats five mediocre ones
4. **Dark and confident** — a dark palette sets us apart from every other green gardening brand on the market
5. **Show the why** — always explain why an ingredient or ratio exists, not just that it's "premium"

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. All text meets ≥4.5:1 contrast on dark backgrounds. Focus indicators visible. Reduced motion respected via CSS media query. Semantic HTML throughout. Alt text on all product imagery.
